📖 Rules of the jungle (How to play?)

  • Fill the board: In each row and in each column there must be exactly one beast of each size (from 1 to the size of the board). Numbers do not repeat!
  • Understand the clues: Numbers on the outside of the board are clues. They tell you how many beasts you can see looking into the given row or column.
  • Visibility rule: A higher beast blocks all smaller beasts behind it! If you see a clue "1", it means the largest beast is at the edge.
  • Speed matters: Start the timer, press Enter, to submit your result and climb the leaderboard.
